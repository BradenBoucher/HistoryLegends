History Legends

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how History Legends ("we", "our", or "the game") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the game at historylegends-80b84.web.app.

Information We Collect

To provide accounts and save your progress, we collect:

  • Email address — used as your unique account identifier and for sign-in.
  • Password — never seen or stored by us in readable form. Authentication is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication, which stores only a securely hashed version.
  • Commander name (display name) — a name you choose when signing up.
  • Game progress — coins earned, battles completed, upgrades purchased, units unlocked, and incorrectly answered questions (used to re-test you on boss battles).

We do not collect or request: your real name, age, address, phone number, payment information, location, or any data outside what is described above.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To save and load your game progress across sessions and devices.
  • To deliver the educational gameplay experience.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Account credentials and game progress are stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase is operated by Google LLC and may store data on servers in the United States or other countries where Google maintains infrastructure. Google's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Cookies and Local Storage

The game uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits. This is essential to the game functioning and is not used for advertising or tracking. No third-party advertising cookies are set.

Children's Privacy

History Legends is an educational tool intended primarily for high-school and college-aged learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us using the information below and we will delete the account and its data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data associated with your account.
  • Correct inaccurate information by editing your profile or contacting us.
  • Delete your account and all associated game progress.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your authentication record and game progress are removed promptly thereafter.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the game after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, account deletion requests, or any other concerns, please contact: b1boucher@student.bridgew.edu
